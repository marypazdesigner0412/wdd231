TUYI2 Fit

Luzmary Paz de Hernández – Project Site Plan

Site Name

Selected Name: TUYI2 Fit

Reasoning: This name directly commercializes and formalizes the established identity of the community fitness group. Keeping "TUYI2" maintains brand recognition among existing informal trainees while appending "Fit" instantly communicates the athletic, dynamic purpose of the business to prospective clients.

Optional Domain: tuyi2fit.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of the TUYI2 Fit website is to serve as a high-conversion, dynamic hub that transitions informal community trainees into registered, paying gym members. The site will establish brand credibility, showcase coaching methodology/schedules, highlight community success stories, and provide a streamlined, interactive registration funnel for newcomers.

Target Audience Scenarios

The following user stories/questions drive the content priority for the website design:

  • Scenario 1: "I currently train casually with the TUYI2 community group, but how do I officially sign up for the premium coached sessions, and what are the membership rates?"
  • Scenario 2: "I am looking for a structured functional fitness program in my area. What does a typical class layout look like at TUYI2 Fit, and what are the training hours?"

Color Scheme

This site plan document utilizes the exact CSS variables and color palettes selected below to reflect the intense, high-energy environment of a functional fitness Box.

#111827

Primary Dark (Charcoal/Black): Used for dominant layouts, main headers, footers, and text blocks to establish a sleek, modern, gritty aesthetic.

#CCFF00

Accent Color (Volt/Neon Green): Used sparingly but intentionally for high-priority Call-to-Actions (CTAs), heading highlights, active navigational states, and key buttons to draw immediate attention.

#F9FAFB

Light Text/Background: High contrast canvas background alternatives to maximize content readability.

Typography

To strike a balance between aggressive athletic branding and clean, accessible user interactions, two Google Fonts have been chosen:

  • Oswald (Weights: 500, 700)
    Applied to: All major headings (H1, H2, H3) and structural navigation links. Its condensed, strong sans-serif characteristics give off an energetic, powerful fitness vibe.
  • Montserrat (Weights: 400, 700)
    Applied to: General body content, forms, buttons, and text descriptions. Highly geometric and readable across multiple screen sizes.
